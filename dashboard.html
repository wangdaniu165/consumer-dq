Piecewise fit uses 3 knots (6.0%, 6.4%, 8.8%) with the monotonicity constraint (all segment slopes ≥ 0). Stress test holds or steps unemployment and projects 8 quarters. λ ≈ 0 means no cointegration — the first-difference (ECM) model is the sound read. See docs/model.md for full methodology.
